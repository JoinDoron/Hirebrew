PHASE 1: ALIGN
Work: Map the workflow, stakeholder responsibilities and systems of record.
Deliverable: Signed-off scope, baseline and success measures.
Gate: Business owner and implementation lead agree what is being tested.
Owner: _________________________ Target date: ____________________________

PHASE 2: VALIDATE
Work: Test the complete workflow with sample data. Verify access, privacy, security, audit, integrations and recovery. Obtain appropriate review for each operating market, including Nigeria where relevant.
Deliverable: Readiness evidence, issue log and approved data-handling plan.
Gate: No real people data until required controls, contracts and approvals are in place.
Owner: _________________________ Target date: ____________________________

PHASE 3: PILOT
Work: Introduce an approved cohort. Train users, monitor critical journeys and capture feedback. Agree support coverage and an escalation route.
Deliverable: Adoption evidence, measured outcomes and prioritised issues.
Gate: Review against the original acceptance criteria before expansion.
Owner: _________________________ Target date: ____________________________

PHASE 4: EXPAND
Work: Make an explicit go or no-go decision. Schedule additional teams and workflows only when readiness has been demonstrated.
Deliverable: Approved expansion plan, owners and ongoing operating cadence.
Gate: Open risks accepted by accountable owners or resolved before release.
Owner: _________________________ Target date: ____________________________

Payroll history does not imply payroll calculation, tax filing or payment execution. AI assistance requires human oversight.
