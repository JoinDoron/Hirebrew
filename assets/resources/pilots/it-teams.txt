HIREBREW PILOT BRIEF
IT teams

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
From product walkthrough to deployment evidence.
Choose a narrow employee journey, then test both the visible experience and the controls that must support it in a real deployment.

HANDOFFS
Inspect | IT + process owners
Walk every participating role
Follow a recruiter, candidate, interviewer and manager through the same sample journey. Capture the minimum information and actions each participant actually requires.

Design | Identity + security owners
Define access before live use
Specify identity provider, MFA, SSO and permission expectations. Include denied-access tests and account lifecycle requirements rather than checking only successful sign-in.

Connect | Integration owners
Treat interfaces as individual workstreams
Agree source systems, provider credentials, field mappings and error ownership. Validate each proposed exchange with sample records before relying on it operationally.

Operate | Delivery + support owners
Prove recovery and escalation paths
Establish monitoring, backup and incident tests appropriate to the deployment. Document who responds when a workflow or external delivery fails during the pilot.

PILOT SCOPE
A narrow scope with meaningful failure tests.
Include the devices, connectivity and locations your teams actually use. Test a Nigerian branch or field setting where relevant, not only a head-office desktop.

VALIDATION CHECKLIST
[ ] Denied-access and account lifecycle cases are specified
[ ] Integration errors have a responsible owner
[ ] Recovery and monitoring evidence is reviewed

BOUNDARIES
Production identity, SSO, MFA, strict permissions and operational assurance require implementation and verification; this preview does not establish them.
