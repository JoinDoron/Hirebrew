HANDOFFS
Request | Operations lead + HR
Agree on the requirement
Describe the roles, approver and interview criteria before recruiting begins. Use a sample requisition to check whether the operational need is understood by the hiring team.

Select | Recruiters + interviewers
Keep selection and communication together
Explore candidate stages, panel feedback and two-way messages. Name the person responsible for responding when a candidate asks about the next step.

Prepare | Team lead + new starters
Assign the first-week essentials
Map onboarding tasks and learning responsibilities for each sample new hire. Review what must happen outside Hirebrew, including specialist access or operational readiness checks.

Support | Employee + HR + manager
Keep ownership after the cohort joins
Follow a leave or letter request into the employee-service journey. Use an early check-in to identify learning needs rather than treating onboarding completion as the end.

PILOT SCOPE
Test a small cohort before testing volume.
Include recruiters, a team lead and sample employee users. Observe the experience on the devices and connections people expect to use.
