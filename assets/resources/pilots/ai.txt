HANDOFFS
Scope | Technical lead + HR
Name the problem the hire will solve
Connect the role request to specific team needs. Agree on essential skills and the parts of the job that the employee can learn after joining.

Evaluate | Engineering + product panel
Bring complementary evidence together
Use scorecards to capture technical judgement, collaboration and practical problem-solving. Keep candidate communication open while the panel resolves differing views.

Onboard | Manager + new hire
Set up the first learning loop
Explore first-week tasks and a 30/60/90-day plan. Assign introductions and learning responsibilities while infrastructure and repository permissions stay with their existing owners.

Develop | Manager + employee
Revisit goals as the work changes
Use check-ins and development conversations to discuss progress and emerging skill needs. Keep the employee's growth visible alongside a changing product or research roadmap.

PILOT SCOPE
Evaluate the people workflow like a product.
Choose one specialist role and a sample-data journey. Inspect the handoffs and define acceptance criteria before broadening the pilot.
