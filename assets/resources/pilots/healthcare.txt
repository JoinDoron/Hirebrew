Healthcare

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
A considered handoff into a department.
A sample administrative role is a useful first pilot. It tests the people process without requiring sensitive clinical or patient data.

HANDOFFS
Define | Department lead + HR
Agree the role and approval
Set out the vacancy and role expectations. Confirm the people responsible for approving the requisition before candidates enter the pipeline.

Assess | Interview panel
Record relevant evidence
Coordinate the panel and capture structured scorecards. Keep candidate communication two-way so unanswered practical questions are visible before an offer.

Prepare | Onboarding coordinator
Name the handoff owners
Assign introductory and administrative tasks. Record who must coordinate with specialist verification teams without suggesting that Hirebrew performs their checks.

Follow through | Manager + employee
Make early support deliberate
Use check-ins and development goals to discuss role clarity and learning needs. Explore a sample employee request to test the ongoing support route.

PILOT SCOPE
Begin with a low-risk workforce scenario.
Bring HR, a departmental representative and your privacy owner into a sample-data walkthrough. Agree the boundary between people administration and specialist clinical responsibilities.

BOUNDARIES
The preview does not provide patient records, clinical rostering, credential verification or health-information compliance certification. Production security and integrations require validation.

AI EVALUATION
Brew AI's illustrative drafting can help organise an administrative onboarding outline using approved role information. A responsible manager reviews every suggestion. Do not include patient details, medical information or professional verification records in the website demo.
Example prompt: Draft a welcome checklist for a hospital administration assistant. Include introductions and an early manager check-in. Exclude patient data, clinical guidance and credential decisions.
