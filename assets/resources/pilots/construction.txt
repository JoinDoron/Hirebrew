HIREBREW PILOT BRIEF
Construction

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
From project need to an owned welcome.
Walk through a sample quantity surveyor vacancy with HR and one project team before considering a wider site rollout.

HANDOFFS
Scope | Project lead
Set out the vacancy
Describe the role and request the required approval. Align the hiring brief with the project team's needs before moving into candidate selection.

Select | HR + technical interviewer
Compare relevant experience
Coordinate candidate stages and interview participation. Use scorecards to capture evidence, then answer candidate questions through the demonstrated messaging flow.

Welcome | Site manager
Coordinate the people handoff
Assign introductions, administrative welcome tasks and first-week conversations. Keep mandatory site approvals with the competent teams who own them.

Review | Manager + employee
Check the first weeks
Use goals and check-ins to clarify expectations and learning needs. Explore requests and letters as part of the employee's ongoing support experience.

PILOT SCOPE
Start with one project team.
Use a sample professional or support role to trace the full hiring handoff. Include a site participant so the evaluation reflects working conditions outside head office.

VALIDATION CHECKLIST
[ ] Agree the approval route between project leadership and central HR.
[ ] Test mobile access and realistic connectivity at the selected location.
[ ] Identify where safety, contractor and site-access processes remain separate.

BOUNDARIES
Site safety certification, contractor compliance, timesheets, labour rostering and project cost accounting are outside this preview. Live integrations and production permissions require validation.
