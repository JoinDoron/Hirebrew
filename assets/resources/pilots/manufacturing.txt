HIREBREW PILOT BRIEF
Manufacturing

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
From a site vacancy to a supported starter.
A proposed pilot for one plant and one job family. Use sample records to test where central and local responsibilities meet.

HANDOFFS
Request | Plant manager
Describe the vacancy
Raise a requisition with the location and role requirements. Route the request through the agreed approval path before HR opens the candidate pipeline.

Select | HR + supervisor
Compare the evidence
Coordinate candidate stages, panel participation and structured feedback. Use two-way messaging to resolve candidate questions before the next step.

Prepare | Site onboarding owner
Assign the first-week tasks
Make the welcome, introductions and local onboarding responsibilities visible. Keep specialist safety sign-off with the qualified team and its established system.

Support | Manager + employee
Keep the conversation going
Explore leave, letters and employee requests alongside development goals. Use early check-ins to identify where a new colleague still needs support.

PILOT SCOPE
Start with one plant, not your whole operation.
Walk through a sample vacancy with a supervisor, recruiter and employee representative. Agree what successful ownership looks like before expanding to other sites.

BOUNDARIES
This preview does not provide shift rostering, time-and-attendance, safety certification or payroll execution. Production access controls and integrations require validation.

AI EVALUATION
Brew AI concepts can help draft a role-specific onboarding outline from approved requirements. A site leader reviews the language, responsibilities and omissions. The website demonstrates illustrative drafting, not live safety guidance or automated decisions.
Example prompt: Draft a first-week welcome plan for a maintenance technician. Include manager introductions and learning discussions. Leave safety authorisation to the site's approved process.
