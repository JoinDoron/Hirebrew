HIREBREW PILOT BRIEF
Fintech

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
A clearer path for an operations hire.
Follow a sample hiring request through selection, team readiness and the first review conversation.

HANDOFFS
Approve | Operations lead + HR
Connect the role to a real need
Describe the capacity or capability gap in a requisition. Agree on its approval owner and selection criteria before asking a panel to assess candidates.

Assess | Interview panel
Compare evidence, not impressions
Gather scorecards from operations, product or other relevant participants. Keep the decision human-led and use candidate messages to clarify the next step.

Prepare | HR + manager
Assign first-week responsibilities
Explore an onboarding checklist covering introductions, learning and manager expectations. Identify any access or policy task that must be completed in a specialist system.

Develop | Manager + employee
Continue the conversation after day one
Use goals and check-ins to review the employee's first month. Turn learning needs into a development discussion rather than leaving them in the original interview notes.

PILOT SCOPE
Prove one journey before widening access.
Bring HR, an operations manager and a technology reviewer into a sample-data pilot. Agree on the evidence required before expansion.

VALIDATION CHECKLIST
[ ] Selection criteria and final decision ownership are clear.
[ ] Each onboarding dependency has a named owner.
[ ] Data visibility and retention questions are documented for review.

BOUNDARIES
The preview is not a KYC, AML, transaction-monitoring or certified financial-services compliance system. Production controls need separate verification.
