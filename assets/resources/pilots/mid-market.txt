HIREBREW PILOT BRIEF
Mid-Market

WORKFLOW TO EVALUATE
A shared process. Ownership close to the work.
Evaluate one employee journey across two departments. Their differences will reveal where you need flexibility and where inconsistency adds no value.

HANDOFFS
Align | HR + department leads
Agree the common starting point
Map hiring requests, approval owners and role criteria. Document exceptions so teams can distinguish a genuine requirement from an inherited habit.

Select | Recruiters + interviewers
Collect comparable feedback
Coordinate panel participation and scorecards while keeping the candidate conversation active. Confirm who moves the process forward after each interview.

Support | HR + line managers
Make everyday requests easier to follow
Walk through onboarding, leave and letter requests from both employee and manager perspectives. Check what happens when a request needs another team's input.

Develop | Managers + employees
Connect goals to regular conversations
Explore check-ins, feedback and learning needs. Use the review process to clarify development actions and the person responsible for following through.

PILOT SCOPE
Test two departments, not the whole company.
Choose teams with different hiring or service needs. Include a location outside head office where relevant to your Nigerian or wider African operation.

VALIDATION CHECKLIST
[ ] Common steps and justified exceptions are documented
[ ] Employees and managers can complete the same journey
[ ] A baseline exists for handoff time and completion

BOUNDARIES
Confirm deployment permissions, policy configuration and migration quality before expanding beyond sample records.
