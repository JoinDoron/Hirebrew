Serious about work.
Optimistic about people.

Hirebrew connects a candidate’s first conversation to an employee’s next chapter. The identity should feel like that connection: open, capable and unmistakably human.

Handoff brings two folded bands into one connected form. The open channel between them keeps each part distinct while the interlocking silhouette suggests shared work and forward progress. It represents people supporting one another, from the first conversation to the next opportunity.

Protect the shape.

Use the monochrome version when only one ink is available and the reversed version on ink backgrounds. The compact 22 px mark is reserved for incidental product-demo branding. Never stretch, rotate, outline or add a glow. Avoid placing the logo on detailed imagery.

Warmth.
With a clear signal.

Chalk creates breathing room. Ink gives us conviction. Coral is the moment to act.

Select a swatch to copy its hex value.

Give each colour a job. Keep most of the canvas neutral. Use coral for the main action and small human moments. Periwinkle supports intelligence and connected-work stories, never competing with coral for attention.

Contrast is part of the identity. Ink on coral is 5.71:1. Ink on chalk is 14.76:1. Ink on periwinkle is 9.97:1. Do not use chalk or white for small text on coral.

Use it as a frame, not a filter. The connected bands work on campaign covers, presentation dividers and welcome materials. Crop confidently. Preserve the bands’ proportions and the breathing room between repeats.

Sound like someone
you would work with.

Clear, not corporate.

“Your interview is confirmed.”

Not “Your talent engagement milestone has been successfully facilitated.”

Helpful, not all-knowing.

“Here’s a draft. Make it yours.”

Not “AI has found the perfect candidate.” AI assists. People make the decisions.

Optimistic, not overclaimed.

“Build your team’s next chapter.”

Not “Guaranteed to transform every hire.” Be specific about what works today.

A visual language
that respects people.

The experience matters more than the decoration. Every visual choice should make work easier to understand.

Product first

Show real screens and actual workflows. Label concept visuals. Do not invent customers, outcomes or compliance badges.

Focus is visible

Use clear keyboard outlines and 44 px interaction targets. Rail tooltips use bold white text on an ink background, visible on hover and focus. The active rail item has a translucent grey outline.

Motion has a purpose

Use 180 ms for control feedback. Reserve longer transitions for explaining a change. Honour reduced-motion preferences. No decorative endless animation.

People, with agency

Use consensual, real-world African photography when available. Show people doing meaningful work. Avoid stock diversity theatre and robot-led AI imagery.

Learn from the best.
Look like ourselves.

Principles from leading product websites, translated for Hirebrew. The structure informs us. Their identity remains theirs.

ReferenceWhat the site doesOur design decision

Stripe ↗

Explains a broad platform through business solutions, customer stories and product-specific next steps.

Begin with the outcome. Reveal the connected workflow before introducing the full module list.

Linear ↗

Places product interactions alongside the stages of planning and building.

Let people explore the work, not just read feature claims. Keep interface detail purposeful and legible.

Rippling ↗

Connects its app portfolio to shared people data, permissions and concrete AI use cases.

Tell one connected people story. Explain what AI assists with and where a person stays in control.

Deel ↗

Opens with tasks visitors recognise, then groups payroll, HR, hiring and other solutions.

Help visitors find their role and task quickly. A candidate and an HR leader should not need the same introduction.

Shopify ↗

Pairs entrepreneurial ambition with practical operations, varied business stages and an AI assistant.

Keep the future optimistic but concrete. Show the everyday work that makes a bigger ambition possible.

Apple ↗

Uses focused product stories, short copy and distinct discovery and purchase actions.

Give each section one job. Separate exploring the product from committing to a conversation.

Flutterwave ↗

Connects an expansive promise to specific tools for enterprises, small businesses and individuals.

Be African-first through useful context, clear examples and credible scope, not decorative stereotypes.

Official pages reviewed in October 2026. These are observations and design hypotheses, not evidence that a layout caused commercial success. Validate Hirebrew’s design with real customers, task completion and qualified demo interest.
