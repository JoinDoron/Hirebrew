Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Brew generating live AI responses here?+

No. The three website examples use prepared text that you can review and edit locally. They demonstrate the intended interaction, not a connected model or live service.

Does saving a draft send it or update employee records?+

No. Saving only changes the current preview session. It does not send an email, contact a candidate, update the product database or create a production audit record.

What would a live Brew pilot need?+

An agreed provider and task scope, approved data access, security and retention review, accountable reviewers and measured evaluation. Availability and approval enforcement must be verified before real people data or operational use.
