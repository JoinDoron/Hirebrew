The smallest requests can create the longest chase.

An employee needs a letter. A manager needs context for leave. HR needs to know what has changed. When each request starts a separate conversation, finding the right record becomes work of its own. People brings everyday HR into a shared context, understandable to the person asking and the person responding.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does People replace our existing HRIS?+

Not by assumption. Identify authoritative records and the journeys you want to improve. Evaluate migration, ownership and proposed connections before deciding what to replace.

Are leave rules and letters automatically compliant?+

No. Company policies, letter wording and applicable employment requirements need review by your responsible HR and legal advisers. The preview is not a compliance assurance service.

Can we begin with employee requests only?+

Yes. Start with a frequent, well-understood request and a small group of reviewers. Check access, ownership and follow-through before widening into sensitive records or additional workflows.
