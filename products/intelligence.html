Why IntelligenceCapabilitiesUse casesHow it connectsQuestions

A dashboard is only useful if it changes the conversation.

Leadership needs more than a number on a slide. An open role or a stretched department needs context: who is included, which period is compared and what comes next. Intelligence explores these connections across Hirebrew's people journey, turning reporting into a focused decision process.

Start with the business question

Filling approved roles, understanding a department or planning headcount? Give the review a specific purpose before choosing a view or interpreting a measure.

Know what the number includes

Check the populations and periods behind a view. In mixed workforces, a contractor deployment and an employee departure should not automatically tell the same story.

Leave with a responsible owner

Turn a finding into a question for the relevant manager or people partner. Name the person checking evidence and owning the next action.

Inside Hirebrew Intelligence.

Explore the features behind the promise. Agree the scope and deployment requirements before working with real employee data.

Workforce context

Explore prototype roster and worker-type views. Consider how employees, contractors, agency workers and service-year placements should appear in your reporting scope.

Headcount planning views

Bring approved roles into a clearer planning conversation. Define how requested roles, approvals and filled positions relate during pilot scoping.

Hiring visibility

Review sample pipeline views alongside requisitions and candidate stages. Identify where a role needs attention without turning incomplete metrics into recruiter verdicts.

Department perspectives

Use department context to structure a management review. Agree definitions, access boundaries and source freshness before operational use.

Name the decision

Find the relevant context

Review the workforce, headcount or hiring view that addresses the question. Check the period, population and organisational ownership before drawing a conclusion.

Workflow ownerData owner

Workflow ownerAccountable leader

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Are these live operational dashboards?+

The current product and website demonstrate reporting concepts using sample or seeded data. Operational feeds, refresh timing and source reconciliation must be validated for a pilot.

Can Intelligence forecast attrition or hiring outcomes?+

Do not treat illustrative scores or sample views as validated forecasts. Predictive use would need its own evidence, data controls, suitability assessment and accountable human review.

Can we connect existing HR or finance data?+

Specify the systems, fields and update needs. Integration foundations are not evidence of a live connection; validate the exact flow in your pilot.
