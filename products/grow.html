Help a first-time manager coach+

The situationA strong individual contributor now leads a small team across Lagos and another office.

With HirebrewUse shared goals and one-to-one prompts to discuss priorities, capture feedback and agree individual support.

What changesCoaching becomes a repeatable conversation instead of relying on the manager to invent a process.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does Grow replace manager judgement?+

No. Goals, reviews and learning provide context for a conversation. Managers remain responsible for fair decisions, and employees need a clear opportunity to contribute, question and respond.

Can we use our existing review approach?+

Map your cycle, questions and evidence needs to the pilot workflows. Confirm custom scoring, calibration and integrations rather than assuming availability.

Is this a complete learning management system?+

The product includes learning and development experiences. Course standards, external content providers, certification management and mandatory-training rules require separate validation for your organisation.
