Why HireCapabilitiesUse casesHow it connectsQuestions

The role is open. The handoffs should not be.

A promising candidate can wait while a manager approves the role, an interviewer finds their notes or a recruiter chases a reply. Separate trackers rarely show the whole story. Hire brings the stages of selection into a connected workflow, from the reason for hiring to the evidence behind a decision. The aim is a process people can follow, on both sides of the interview.

Start with an agreed role

Put the business need, approval and hiring ownership before the shortlist. Give the recruiter and panel a shared starting point instead of asking them to reconcile changing expectations later.

Make communication two-way

Candidates need a way to ask, clarify and respond. Keep their conversation alongside the hiring journey so the next action is not hidden in someone else's inbox.

Keep decisions grounded in evidence

Bring interviewers into structured feedback around the role. Review different perspectives together, identify missing input and leave the final decision with the accountable hiring team.

Workflow ownerHiring manager + approver

Give the role a clear purpose

Capture the hiring request and its business rationale. Agree who approves the vacancy and what evidence the panel should collect before candidate selection begins.

Workflow ownerRecruiter + candidate

Keep the next step visible

Follow the candidate pipeline and exchange messages. A candidate can ask a question, and the recruiter can respond with context rather than restarting the conversation.

Workflow ownerInterview panel

Bring the right perspectives together

Organise interview participation and structured scorecards. Give technical, functional and people interviewers clear responsibilities, then check which feedback is still needed.

Workflow ownerHiring manager + HR

Make the judgment together

Review the evidence, outstanding questions and candidate conversation before deciding. Carry an agreed selection into onboarding with an explicit owner for the next handoff.

Can candidates reply to HR?+

Yes. The local product tour demonstrates a two-way exchange between candidate and HR views. Use that journey to evaluate response ownership before introducing real applicant information.

How do additional interviewers participate?+

The product includes an interviewer journey and structured scorecards. A live pilot must verify panel membership, invitations, sign-in and the records each interviewer may access.

Can we start with one recruitment team?+

Yes. Begin with one vacancy and an agreed panel. Review the complete candidate journey before adding departments, more locations or integrations.
