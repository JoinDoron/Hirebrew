Confidence comes
from evidence.

Bring your IT, security and people teams into the conversation early. Here is the evidence a rollout should establish.

Identity & permissions

Confirm who can access each portal, which records they can see, and who can approve or export sensitive information.

  • Identity provider, MFA and recovery
  • Server-side roles and record access
  • Joiner, mover and leaver scenarios

Data handling

Map where personal data travels and how it is protected, retained, exported and deleted across the full lifecycle.

  • Hosting, encryption and subprocessors
  • Retention and deletion responsibilities
  • Privacy review for each operating market

Audit & operations

Test that important changes are attributable and that your team can recover from failures, not just notice them.

  • Decision history and export permissions
  • Backup restoration and incident ownership
  • Monitoring and support escalation

Human-led AI

Define where Brew may assist, which source information it can use, and where a person must make the decision.

  • Review before a message or decision
  • Traceable source context and limitations
  • Provider data use and bias evaluation
A verification checklist, not a certification claim.

These controls require implementation evidence and deployment-specific testing before live employee or candidate data is introduced.

Before you
take the next step.

What is available to evaluate today?+

This website is a product and buyer-experience preview, not a production service commitment. The interactive tour uses sample data. Confirm module availability, live integrations, security controls and support terms against a verified deployment before a pilot with real people data.

Do we have to replace our current HR systems?+

No replacement is assumed. Begin with your system of record and the handoffs you want to improve. Integration feasibility, data ownership and migration requirements belong in the initial evaluation.

Can Brew make hiring or performance decisions?+

The product direction is human-led assistance: drafts, summaries and context for review. People remain accountable for hiring, employment and performance decisions. The website's AI examples are scripted, not a live AI service.

How should we evaluate local data requirements?+

Involve your privacy and legal advisers. Document purposes, responsibilities, access, retention, deletion and any cross-border data flows for each market, including Nigeria. This preview does not certify compliance or replace a deployment-specific legal review.

What happens when I build a pilot brief?+

A text file is generated in your browser for you to download and share with your team. Nothing is submitted to Hirebrew. The information is not saved by this page and disappears when you reload.
