What is Hirebrew?+

Hirebrew brings recruiting and people operations into a connected experience. Its product direction spans hiring, candidate communication, interviews, onboarding, employee services, learning and growth.

Who is it built for?+

HR and talent teams, hiring managers, interviewers, candidates and employees. Each has a different job to do, but every handoff should stay connected. Nigerian and African operating contexts inform the design.

Is this a live product account?+

No. This website contains a self-contained interactive concept using sample data. It shows how the experience can work without accessing the operational product, creating accounts or sending messages. The existing product portals are unchanged.

What does Brew AI do?+

The intended role of Brew is to help with drafts, summaries and next steps, while people review consequential decisions. The examples here are scripted. Live AI availability, data access and approval controls must be confirmed for a pilot; this page does not claim they are deployed.

What about email, payroll and other integrations?+

Integration readiness depends on provider configuration and testing. Email, calendar, messaging and payroll or HRIS connections should be scoped and verified during implementation. No third-party connection is activated by this website.

How do we get ready for a pilot?+

Choose one workflow and the people involved, agree what success looks like, then validate identity, permissions, data protection and any integrations. Use the pilot planner to download a starting brief. It stays on your device and is not submitted to a sales team.
