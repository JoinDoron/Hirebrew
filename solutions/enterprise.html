The hard part is what happens between systems.

Enterprise teams rarely lack tools. They lack a shared picture of the handoffs between them. A hiring decision crosses HR, a manager, interviewers, IT and finance before someone can start well. Hirebrew offers a connected workflow concept to evaluate against that operating reality, with procurement questions and deployment requirements treated as part of the work.

Map decisions across the organisation

Trace approval, interview and onboarding ownership across business units and sites. Identify who needs to contribute, who decides and who should not see a record.

Keep existing systems in the picture

Identify the authoritative record for identity, employee data and payroll. Scope any proposed exchange before assuming that two products are connected.

Expand only when evidence supports it

Use a limited pilot to test the employee experience and operational requirements. Set acceptance criteria for adoption, access, reliability and support before increasing scope.

From enterprise ambition to a testable pilot.

Bring HR, IT, legal and finance into discovery together. A convincing demonstration is useful; an agreed evaluation plan makes it actionable.

Turn requirements into evidence

Document identity, record visibility, data location, retention and incident expectations. Define what evidence must exist before real employee information can be introduced.

Workflow ownerDelivery team + managers

Walk the complete journey

Test candidate communication, interviewer participation and manager onboarding responsibilities. Include exception paths, missing approvals and the employee's view of the process.

Workflow ownerSteering group

Make expansion an explicit decision

Review adoption, unresolved risks and integration work against the agreed criteria. Approve the next stage only when owners and responsibilities remain clear.

A few good questions.

Is enterprise SSO already demonstrated?+

No. SSO, MFA and production identity requirements belong in the implementation and verification scope. The local preview should not be used as assurance of those controls.

Does this replace our HRIS?+

That decision should follow system mapping. Start by identifying where Hirebrew could improve a journey, then evaluate ownership, migration and integration options without assuming replacement.

What should procurement request first?+

A scoped pilot brief, a capabilities and limitations review, deployment evidence and a clear cost breakdown. The enterprise resources provide a starting checklist for those conversations.
