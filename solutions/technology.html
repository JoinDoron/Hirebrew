A great candidate experience needs a strong handoff.

A hiring manager opens a role, engineers join interviews and HR handles the candidate conversation. Once someone accepts, onboarding crosses management, people operations and IT. Hirebrew helps you explore that whole journey so the care you put into hiring carries into the first weeks and the employee's development, instead of disappearing between tools and teams.

Keep technical selection structured

Agree the role before hiring, involve the right interviewers and record role-relevant scorecards. Give candidates a two-way communication route while keeping the panel accountable for evidence and the final decision.

Make onboarding a team responsibility

Assign introductions, first-week tasks and early manager check-ins. Clarify the handoff to IT and other owners without pretending a people task automatically provisions accounts or grants production access.

Connect growth to regular conversations

Explore goals, feedback, learning and development needs as teams evolve. Give managers a repeatable structure while leaving room for the differences between engineering, product, design and commercial roles.

A few good questions.

How is this different from the IT industry page?+

This page focuses on companies building technology products and their cross-functional people journey. The IT page addresses technology-services delivery teams; the IT teams solution addresses the internal buyer's responsibilities.

Will it provision accounts for new hires?+

No automatic provisioning is demonstrated. Hirebrew can be evaluated for the onboarding responsibility and handoff, while identity, device and application access remain with your IT tools and owners.

Can Brew AI make hiring decisions for us?+

No. The website demonstrates illustrative drafts for human review. Selection remains a human decision grounded in agreed criteria, interview evidence and your organisation's approved process.
