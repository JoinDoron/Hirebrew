Technical talent needs more than a role title.

A service desk specialist, implementation consultant and infrastructure engineer contribute in different ways. Hiring can become fragmented when delivery managers and HR hold separate pieces of the story. Hirebrew helps you explore a connected journey, where the role's requirements inform the interview and continue into onboarding, goals and learning conversations.

Align recruitment with delivery needs

Use role requests and approval ownership to clarify the capability gap. Bring delivery managers into selection before the candidate reaches the final stage.

Carry context into the first month

Turn hiring expectations into named onboarding tasks and early goals. Make the manager responsible for the transition, with specialist access work handled in existing systems.

Keep skills development visible

Explore learning, check-ins and internal opportunities for growing technical teams. Discuss the skills needed for future responsibilities, not only the vacancy that brought someone in.

A technical hire with a connected next chapter.

Follow a sample implementation consultant from the approved requirement to an early development conversation.

Illustrative workflow. Explore the ownership at each handoff.

Workflow ownerDelivery manager + HR

Describe the capability you need

Create a requisition around technical and client-facing requirements. Identify the approver and separate essential evidence from skills that can be developed after joining.

Workflow ownerTechnical + delivery panel

Evaluate more than one dimension

Use structured scorecards to organise technical reasoning, communication and collaboration. Review the evidence together while maintaining a clear two-way candidate conversation.

Workflow ownerManager + new employee

Agree on the first assignments to learn

Explore onboarding tasks and a first-month plan. Name owners for introductions and learning, while device setup, identity and customer access remain in their approved systems.

Workflow ownerEmployee + manager

Turn experience into a development plan

Use check-ins and goals to identify learning needs and future opportunities. For teams serving clients across African markets, discuss the location and working-context requirements explicitly.
