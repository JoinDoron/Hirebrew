Your mission needs ownership, not more chasing.

A programme vacancy can cross a country office, a functional lead and central HR before recruitment begins. Once a colleague joins, another set of people owns introductions and support. Hirebrew helps you explore a connected staff journey, making those responsibilities visible without turning a people platform into a donor, grant or beneficiary management system.

Make programme hiring intentional

Capture the role and hiring rationale through a requisition. Bring programme and functional perspectives into structured interviews, with candidate questions handled through a two-way conversation rather than an unanswered email chain.

Give distributed staff a clear welcome

Assign introductions, role orientation and early manager check-ins. A shared task structure helps country, field and central teams see where their responsibilities begin without pretending every operating context is the same.

Keep development close to the work

Explore goals, learning and feedback alongside everyday employee requests. Give staff and managers a place to discuss the support needed for their role, while keeping beneficiary information outside the people workflow.

A few good questions.

Is Hirebrew a donor or grant management tool?+

No. The focus is staff recruitment and the employee journey. Keep donor relationships, grant accounting and programme reporting in specialist systems; any integration must be separately scoped and validated.

Can field teams participate?+

Include them in the evaluation, using sample data and realistic devices. Test connectivity, support language and access needs carefully. Offline operation is not a demonstrated capability.

Does it cover volunteers and safeguarding checks?+

Dedicated volunteer management and safeguarding verification are not demonstrated. Start with an employee workflow, retain approved specialist processes and document other requirements before expanding the scope.
