Your brand should feel familiar behind the counter too.

A new branch, a busy trading period or a departing colleague can turn a store vacancy into an urgent request. Central HR needs visibility, managers need a practical process and candidates need answers. Hirebrew connects those conversations so a promising candidate does not become an unclear handoff when the offer is accepted.

Give store hiring a shared path

Start with a requisition and carry the role into a visible candidate pipeline. Local managers can contribute interview evidence while HR keeps the overall process moving across the store group.

Make the welcome repeatable

Turn the first week into owned tasks, from introductions to role expectations. A consistent structure gives each manager a useful starting point while leaving space for the realities of their location.

Bring support closer to employees

Explore employee requests, letters and leave in a dedicated people experience. Give routine needs a clear place to start, so store managers are not the only bridge between employees and head office.

A few good questions.

Can this support a growing store network?+

Hirebrew's requisitions, employee records and manager workflows provide a useful starting point. A pilot should validate your location model, approval paths and access rules before extending the process.

Will it build staff rotas or connect to our POS?+

Those are outside the demonstrated feature set. Keep existing specialist tools and document any required integrations as separate rollout work, rather than treating them as ready-made connections.

How should we evaluate frontline access?+

Test with store employees, not just head office. Include shared-device policies, mobile data costs and realistic connectivity conditions for your locations in Nigeria and other markets.
