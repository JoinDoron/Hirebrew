Your next hire should not need another spreadsheet.

In a small company, the founder approves the role, a team lead interviews and someone in operations organises day one. When those handoffs live in separate conversations, candidates wait and new starters arrive without a plan. Hirebrew brings the people journey into focus, so a lean team can establish a useful operating rhythm early.

Hire with a shared brief

Agree what the role exists to achieve before interviews begin. Requisitions and approval steps give the hiring team a common starting point.

Keep the conversation moving

Explore a candidate pipeline with two-way messages. Candidates can respond, and the hiring team can see what needs attention next.

Make the first month count

Connect onboarding tasks, manager ownership and early goals. A small team still deserves a considered welcome and clear expectations.

From an open role to a confident start.

Start with the next role you genuinely need. Keep the process small enough for everyone involved to understand and use.

Illustrative workflow. Explore the ownership at each handoff.

Workflow ownerFounder + hiring lead

Approve a real business need

Capture the purpose of the role, decision owner and interview criteria before sourcing begins. Avoid changing the brief halfway through selection.

Workflow ownerRecruiter + candidate

Keep both sides informed

Follow candidate stages and exchange messages around the next step. Test the experience from the candidate's side, not only the recruiter's.

Workflow ownerInterview panel

Compare evidence, not memory

Bring interviewers into the process and collect structured scorecards. Keep the final hiring decision with the people responsible for the role.

Workflow ownerManager + new starter

Carry the context into day one

Assign first-week tasks and a first-month plan. Make introductions, responsibilities and the first check-in explicit before the new hire starts.

A few good questions.

Do we need a full HR department?+

No large department is assumed in this starting point. Assign the recruiter, interviewer and manager responsibilities to the people doing that work, then evaluate whether the process fits their capacity.

Can we begin with hiring only?+

Yes. Scope the evaluation to one requisition, candidate conversations and interview feedback. Add onboarding when the hiring handoff is clear.

Does this include payroll?+

Payroll calculation, tax filing and payment execution are not demonstrated capabilities. Keep your existing provider and document any future data handoff separately.
