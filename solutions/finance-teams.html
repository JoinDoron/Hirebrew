A people decision becomes a finance question quickly.

A new role starts as a departmental request, but its implications reach finance long before the employee arrives. Who approved it? Which system owns the record? What will implementation and ongoing support cost? Hirebrew's finance starting point brings those questions into workflow evaluation, without presenting a hiring platform as a payroll engine.

Clarify the headcount conversation

Explore requisition approval and workforce views alongside the business reason for a role. Identify how finance participates in the decision before recruitment begins.

Know which record to trust

Map employee information, change ownership and payroll-history requirements against existing systems. Separate the source of a field from the destination that uses it.

Evaluate the full cost of change

Consider configuration, migration, provider charges, internal effort and ongoing support. A pilot should expose these components, not hide them behind a single headline price.

A few good questions.

Will Hirebrew run payroll?+

Payroll execution is not represented as an available capability. Evaluate people-data workflows alongside your existing provider and scope any connection separately.

Are the workforce numbers live?+

The marketing and product previews contain illustrative records. Production data sources, refresh rules and reconciliations must be established before using views for business decisions.

Can you promise a return on investment?+

No universal savings figure is claimed. Agree your baseline, internal effort and decision criteria, then use the pilot to assess value in your own organisation.
