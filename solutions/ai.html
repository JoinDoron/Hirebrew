Exceptional specialists need a shared team context.

Hiring a researcher, applied engineer or solutions lead involves different kinds of evidence. Fast-changing role requirements make it tempting to improvise every interview. Hirebrew helps you explore a more deliberate process, where the team agrees on the work, assesses relevant skills and carries those expectations into the new employee's first months.

Define the role before the search

Separate research depth, production engineering and customer problem-solving. Use the requisition and panel criteria to make the actual capability gap clear to everyone involved.

Keep evaluation human and explainable

Collect structured interviewer feedback and review the evidence together. Candidate selection should remain an accountable team decision, not an opaque model score.

Connect joining with contribution

Organise onboarding tasks, manager check-ins and early goals. For teams distributed across African markets and beyond, make ownership explicit rather than assuming proximity will fill the gaps.

Illustrative workflow. Explore the ownership at each handoff.

Workflow ownerTechnical lead + HR

Name the problem the hire will solve

Connect the role request to specific team needs. Agree on essential skills and the parts of the job that the employee can learn after joining.

Workflow ownerEngineering + product panel

Bring complementary evidence together

Use scorecards to capture technical judgement, collaboration and practical problem-solving. Keep candidate communication open while the panel resolves differing views.

Workflow ownerManager + new hire

Set up the first learning loop

Explore first-week tasks and a 30/60/90-day plan. Assign introductions and learning responsibilities while infrastructure and repository permissions stay with their existing owners.

Workflow ownerManager + employee

Revisit goals as the work changes

Use check-ins and development conversations to discuss progress and emerging skill needs. Keep the employee's growth visible alongside a changing product or research roadmap.

A few good questions.

Is Brew connected to a live AI provider?+

Not in this website preview. Its examples illustrate drafting and review interactions. Provider setup and production safeguards require a separate implementation.

Can we include research and commercial interviewers?+

The panel journey lets you explore different participant perspectives. Define each person's criteria and validate role access as part of the pilot.

Does Hirebrew replace engineering tooling?+

No. Model development, compute, repositories and experiment tracking stay in your specialist tools. Hirebrew focuses on the people doing that work.
