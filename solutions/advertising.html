The best creative teams are not one discipline.

An agency hire may meet a creative director, strategist and account lead, each looking for something different. When their feedback stays in separate conversations, the hiring decision becomes harder to explain. Hirebrew gives you a way to explore shared selection, manager handoffs and development without pretending that every discipline needs the same criteria.

Connect different points of view

Agree on the role's core criteria and give each interviewer a clear responsibility. Preserve discipline-specific evidence while bringing the final conversation into one process.

Make the welcome more than logistics

Prepare introductions, first-week learning and manager expectations before a new employee joins a busy team. Keep client-sensitive materials in their approved systems.

Keep growth on the agenda

Explore goals, feedback and learning for creative, strategy and account teams. Give development an owner even when the next client deadline is already approaching.

A few good questions.

Can different disciplines use different criteria?+

Use the pilot to define the evidence each interviewer needs for the role. Test how those perspectives come together before standardising the process.

Does this manage freelance projects?+

No. This page focuses on employee hiring and development. Freelance contracts, project staffing and client billing require separate scoping and systems.

Will Hirebrew manage our campaign work?+

No. Keep production and campaign delivery in your existing tools. Evaluate Hirebrew for the employee journey that supports those teams.
