An appointment letter is only one part of readiness.

Recruiting a colleague can involve HR, a department lead, interviewers and an onboarding owner before the person ever joins a team. Those responsibilities need to meet without confusing administrative progress with clinical approval. Hirebrew focuses on the people journey: clear hiring stages, structured conversations and tasks whose owners know what comes next.

Give each role an intentional process

Use requisitions and structured interview feedback to distinguish what matters for each vacancy. Bring the appropriate departmental voices into selection without relying on a single recruiter to remember every decision.

Separate tasks from specialist sign-off

Coordinate introductions, employee documentation tasks and early check-ins. Keep professional credential checks and authorisation decisions with qualified owners and established specialist systems, rather than treating a checked task as clinical clearance.

Support employees beyond joining day

Explore leave, letters, employee requests and development conversations for clinical and non-clinical teams. Give people-service requests an identifiable route while keeping patient information outside the workforce workflow.

A considered handoff into a department.

A sample administrative role is a useful first pilot. It tests the people process without requiring sensitive clinical or patient data.

A few good questions.

Is Hirebrew a clinical or patient management system?+

No. It is a people-workflow product. Patient records, clinical care and medical decision-making belong in the specialist systems and processes your organisation has approved.

Does it verify professional credentials?+

Credential verification is outside the demonstrated scope. A pilot can clarify who owns related onboarding responsibilities, but it must not treat task completion as proof of professional authorisation.

Can we start with support functions?+

Yes. HR, administration and other non-clinical roles offer a practical evaluation starting point. Use sample data and test the department-to-HR handoffs before considering a broader workforce rollout.
