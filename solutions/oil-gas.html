The handoff matters as much as the location.

Office-based HR, operational managers and site teams can see different parts of an employee's journey. A role may be approved centrally while local teams own essential preparation. Hirebrew helps you evaluate those people-process handoffs, making responsibility explicit without confusing an onboarding checklist with permission to perform safety-critical work.

Bring site needs into role approval

Connect the hiring request to the operational requirement and its approver. Include relevant technical and site stakeholders when defining interview criteria.

Separate people tasks from clearance

Explore onboarding ownership for introductions, learning and employee preparation. Keep permits, medical fitness and safety authorisations in the specialist processes that govern them.

Include employees beyond head office

Evaluate leave, letters and routine requests with the people who will use them. Test connectivity, device access and local support needs across Nigerian and other operating locations.

Follow the journey from approval to site support.

Use a sample operational support role to test coordination between central HR, a manager and the relevant location.

Illustrative workflow. Explore the ownership at each handoff.

Workflow ownerOperations manager + HR

Make the local requirement clear

Create the role request with explicit ownership and selection criteria. Agree on who approves the hire and which stakeholders need to participate in the interview.

Workflow ownerTechnical + operational panel

Bring the right evidence into view

Explore structured scorecards and a shared candidate stage. Use two-way messages for process questions while any specialist qualification verification remains a separate requirement.

Workflow ownerHR + manager + site owner

Map the onboarding responsibilities

Assign people-related tasks and identify dependencies handled elsewhere. A completed Hirebrew checklist must not be treated as a safety clearance or authorisation to enter an operational area.

Workflow ownerEmployee + HR

Keep the route to help clear

Explore how an employee requests a letter or leave and who follows up. Include local access conditions in testing, then use manager check-ins to discuss the first-month experience.

A few good questions.

Does an onboarding checklist authorise site access?+

No. Site access, competence and safety clearance must remain subject to your specialist systems, responsible personnel and approved procedures.

Will it work at locations with limited connectivity?+

That needs testing with your actual devices and connections. The preview does not demonstrate offline access or guaranteed service availability.

Can it manage rotations or contractor compliance?+

Those capabilities are not demonstrated. Scope employment models, rotations and contractor controls separately before deciding how any people-data handoff should work.
