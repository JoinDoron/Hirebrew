A studio is a meeting of different crafts.

A strong portfolio, technical judgement and collaboration are not interchangeable signals. Studios need a hiring process that respects those differences while reaching a shared decision. Hirebrew helps you explore how interview feedback, onboarding responsibilities and development conversations can connect around the person joining the team, not just the next production milestone.

Respect each discipline's evidence

Agree on what art, design, engineering or production interviewers should assess. Use structured feedback to support the discussion without reducing creative judgement to a single number.

Make joining the studio legible

Connect introductions, learning tasks and manager expectations in a first-month plan. Keep engines, source-control permissions and specialist tools with the teams that administer them.

Create room for long-term growth

Explore goals and check-ins that continue beyond a project deadline. Give employees a clearer way to discuss learning needs, role development and future responsibilities.

A few good questions.

Can this work for an independent studio?+

Start with one role and a small panel. The pilot can test a lightweight process before adding broader employee-service or development workflows.

Does Hirebrew manage game production?+

No. Keep milestones, assets and production planning in your specialist tools. Hirebrew focuses on hiring and supporting the people who use them.

Does it assess portfolios automatically?+

No automatic portfolio assessment is demonstrated. Keep creative and technical evaluation with accountable interviewers using agreed criteria.
