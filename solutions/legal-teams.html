The right review happens before the rollout.

People systems touch sensitive information, employment decisions and documents that carry real consequences. Legal teams need more than a generic compliance badge: they need to know what information is collected, who can use it and what happens next. Hirebrew's evaluation approach makes those questions part of workflow design, with clear boundaries between a useful process and a verified control.

Trace the personal-data journey

Follow candidate and employee information from collection through internal sharing. Identify the purpose, responsible owner and required retention or deletion treatment for each touchpoint.

Keep policy content under review

Explore letters, requests and employment-document workflows using approved sample content. Decide who can draft, approve and issue material, and which changes require renewed review.

Make decision accountability explicit

Set boundaries around interview feedback, employee cases and proposed AI assistance. Identify the people responsible for decisions and the evidence they need to retain.

Review the journey, not just the policy document.

Use a sample candidate-to-employee journey to identify the points at which policy, privacy or employment questions become operational requirements.

Illustrative workflow. Explore the ownership at each handoff.

Workflow ownerLegal + HR

Identify what information enters the process

List candidate and employee fields, the purpose for collecting them and the teams that request access. Keep unnecessary personal information out of evaluation datasets.

Workflow ownerPolicy owners

Name the approval responsibilities

Review letters, requests and case touchpoints. Define who owns templates, reviews sensitive changes and can authorise a final communication in the intended deployment.

Workflow ownerIT + privacy reviewers

Ask for evidence of controls

Turn access, retention, deletion and audit requirements into deployment tests. A visible interface or stated policy is not sufficient evidence that a control is enforced.

Workflow ownerBusiness + legal sponsors

Set conditions for live use

Record unresolved questions, permitted pilot data and any required approvals. Revisit the assessment when a new market, provider or AI workflow changes the proposed use.

A few good questions.

Is Hirebrew certified compliant?+

No certification claim is made in this preview. Request deployment-specific evidence and assess the proposed processing against your organisation's applicable obligations.

Are letters automatically legally approved?+

No. Your authorised reviewers must approve content, templates and issuance rules. A document workflow does not establish the legal suitability of its wording.

Can we define deletion requirements?+

Yes, include them in the evaluation brief. The required behaviour, exceptions, responsible parties and test evidence must be agreed before live data is introduced.
